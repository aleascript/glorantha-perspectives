title: "Animism: the world of spirits"
---

![World of spirits](../../../assets/rules/runes/mondes/spirits.png)

> An animist feels the world. They feel the link between the invisible and visible worlds. That is how they can interpret, decide, act. Animists are between two worlds: the mundane and the spiritual. That is their worldview. Always. All the time.

*Derogatory names for others: primitives, savages, ...*

**Resolution rules**

> When the path fades, we call upon the spirits of the place and our invisible allies, then we listen to see if the world chooses to answer us rather than the threat.

- The even dice (2, 4, 6) count as a success (the spirits answer).
- **The Spiritual Pact (Double odd):** The odd dice represent local spirits, erratic by nature. However, if two dice show exactly the same odd face (e.g., two "3s" or two "5s"), together they form 1 success. This reflects a spiritual resonance between the character's own spiritual strength (one of the dice) and a specific spirit (the other die). A temporary pact is then sealed to gain support. Orphaned odds remain indifferent.
- **The Fetish:** The relationship with spirits is free and spontaneous. It is possible to bind a spirit into a fetish (or conversely, suffer the departure of a bound spirit), but this only happens in the case of a decisive narrative outcome. This requires achieving an **Exploit** (to bind) or the occurrence of a **Fiasco** (the spirit flees).

**Power modes**

- **Weakened** mode: The link with the spirit world is broken. Spiritual pacts become impossible to seal: "double odds" no longer count and generate no successes. Only the independent spirits (the even dice) continue to manifest.
- **Heroic** mode: Spiritual alignment. If the lowest die and the highest die rolled share the same parity (both even or both odd), the character gets a total success on **all** their stakes.

**Comments**

When an animist faces a difficulty, they seek to channel the energies of surrounding spirits or spirits in their possession to confront it. They therefore only perceive spirits that can help them, some are difficult to convince, some remain indifferent. The dice merely represent this spiritual barter taking place behind the scenes.

The specific focus on odd rolls represents the character's permanent attention turned towards the spirit world.

We could almost see each die as representing a spirit.
