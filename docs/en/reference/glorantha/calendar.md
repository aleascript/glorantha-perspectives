# Random Calendar

It can be useful to quickly determine a day in the Gloranthan calendar, especially when the Lunar cycle or a festival becomes important.

The most widespread calendar divides the year into **days**, **weeks**, and **seasons**, with a special two-week period called **Sacred Time**.

## Day of the week

Roll **2D6**. Read the first die unless you roll a double.

| Result | Day | Lunar landmark |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Freeze | waning crescent |
| 2 | Water | dying moon |
| 3 | Clay | new moon |
| 4 | Winds | waxing crescent |
| 5 | Fire | first quarter |
| 6 | Wild | full moon |
| double | Gods | last quarter |

The day may directly matter for the [Lunar Way](../../rules/moon/), especially outside the Glowline.

## Week

Roll **1D8**.

| D8 | Week |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | Disorder |
| 2 | Harmony |
| 3 | Death |
| 4 | Fertility |
| 5 | Stasis |
| 6 | Movement |
| 7 | Illusion |
| 8 | Truth |

The same D8 can provide a starting point for [Runic Inspiration](../../rules/runes/inspiration/).

## Season

Roll **2D20**. Read the first die unless you roll a double.

| Result | Season | Landmark |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1–4 | Sea | spring |
| 5–8 | Fire | summer |
| 9–12 | Earth | late summer and autumn |
| 13–16 | Dark | winter |
| 17–20 | Storm | turbulent end of winter |
| double | Sacred Time | New Year ritual period |

During Sacred Time, an even double may suggest Luck and an odd double Fate when you use this roll as a source of inspiration.
