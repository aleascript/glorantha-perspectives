# Perspectives

**Perspectives** come into play when a Situation contains genuine uncertainty that the table wants to resolve through play.

They describe what happens where the Protagonists, Time, and uncertainty meet: clarify what the Situation is actually deciding, recognize what matters here and now, turn those truths into Bets, then discover what happens.

The core of resolution remains simple: identify the Bets, roll the dice, and read the result according to the way the side concerned inhabits the world.

The chapters in this section detail this procedure, the worldviews, and the Gloranthan particularities that transform resolution.
