# Perspectives

**Perspectives** come into play when a Situation contains a genuine uncertainty the table wants to play out.

They describe what happens where Protagonists, Time, and uncertainty meet: clarify what the Situation is deciding, recognize what matters here and now, turn those truths into Stakes, then discover what happens.

The heart of resolution remains simple: identify the Stakes, roll the dice, and read the result according to the relevant side's **Worldview**.

> **Situation → Intention → Focus / Zoom → Stakes → Worldview → result → interpretation → transformed Situation**

The chapters in this section detail that procedure, the Worldviews, and the Gloranthan particularities that transform resolution.
