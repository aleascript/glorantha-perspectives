# Influences and transformations

A **Worldview** describes the world the Protagonist believes they inhabit. But Glorantha also contains forces and experiences capable of **altering that reading without simply replacing it**.

These phenomena are therefore not additional Worldviews.

They may bend an existing Worldview, open a breach in its certainties, or durably transform the Protagonist's relationship to the Cosmos.

## Three major cases

- [**Lunar Influence**](../moon/index.md) modulates certain Worldviews according to the Protagonist's relationship with the Red Moon and according to her phases. The Moon does not replace Theism, Animism, or Logic: she passes through them.
- [**Illumination**](../illumination/index.md) does not create a sixth Worldview. It makes the Worldview the character already inhabited irreversibly permeable: they now know it does not exhaust reality.
- [**Chaos**](../chaos/index.md) likewise does not offer another coherent way to read the world. It opens a rupture in cosmic order and transforms whoever accepts its power.

These three cases are not of the same nature, but they share a function in **Glorantha Perspectives**: they show that one's relationship to reality can itself become a changing truth of the fiction.

A Worldview answers the question: **"in what world does this character believe they are acting?"**

Influences and transformations ask: **"what is happening to that very way of inhabiting the world?"**
