# Carrying Time

Carrying Time means making Glorantha live around the Protagonists without deciding their story for them.

The main gesture is simple: **look at the Situation and make what matters live**.

Present enough of the world for players to make real choices. Listen to their intentions. Act for people and powers with goals of their own. Let consequences change what is true. When the outcome becomes uncertain, use the resolution rules.

This section develops procedures for preparation, adjudication, running Situations, and interpreting consequences.
