# Examples of Protagonists

These Portraits show less a "correct character-sheet format" than different ways of bringing someone into view in Glorantha.

They are not mandatory templates. Their organization varies because the same things do not matter in the same way to everyone.

## Hanya of Jillaro

> “We must help the Goddess and preserve civilization.”

![Hanya](/img/narratives/the-lunar-way/heroes/hanya/hanya-drawing-600x.png)

**Woman, 27 — Lunar Theist from Sylila.**

Hanya is a Guardian of Jillaro, a warrior devoted to Hwarin Dalthippa. She serves with a double-edged axe, a bow, mercury armour that does not sink, and a thoroughbred horse.

Yet her Portrait turns on a contradiction more important than her equipment: **she is wholly committed to the cause of the Red Goddess without bearing the Moon Rune.**

### Runes

![](/img/runes/fire.png) **Fire** — discipline, intransigence, haughtiness toward barbarians.

![](/img/runes/harmony.png) **Harmony** — calming tensions, self-restraint, horsemanship.

![](/img/runes/movement.png) **Movement** — speed, cleaving axe blow, urgency: the Empire is in danger.

### Guardian of Jillaro

- great-axe combat;
- forced march;
- going without sleep;
- tactics, archery, horsemanship;
- aesthete, war paint, head shaved in Lunar fashion;
- **virtues:** honour, sacrifice, unwavering faith, beauty, Sylila first;
- **relationships:** the Fort of the Conquering Sister, several Silver Shields.

### Novice of Hwarin Dalthippa

Her magic is subject to the Lunar cycle.

Hanya has particularly retained from the goddess:

- Safe Route;
- See the Long Term;
- Intimidate Barbarians;
- Feel the Enemy's Weakness;
- Cleaving Axe Blow;
- Red Mask of Terror.

#### Mythic teaching — the Daughter's Road Campaign

Among Hwarin's gestures that her teaching gives Hanya to contemplate, the **Daughter's Road Campaign** has an obvious place.

After years of preparation, Hwarin chooses a line of Power through the southern lands and resolves to follow it to her goal. She leaves Jillaro, crosses the bridge where her husband and son lie, mounts a war steed, puts on a red mask, and marks the road with her steps. At Mirin's Cross, a battle of magic brings forth the **New Fire of the Lunar Way**: across the waters of the Black Eel there remains a crystal bridge, a lasting passage for the army that follows her.

For Hanya, this story connects things that a conventional character sheet would easily separate: preparing a road, seeing the long-term plan, building, terrifying resistance, and turning conquest into lasting order. **Safe Route**, **See the Long Term**, and **Red Mask of Terror** are therefore not merely juxtaposed abilities: they are ways for her to understand and extend some of Hwarin's gestures.

Hanya has not, however, received the whole goddess. She is still a novice: her teaching opens certain magical knowledge and ways of acting to her without making her a fully initiated participant in all Lunar mysteries.

Hanya understands Hwarin Dalthippa mainly through **conquest** and the ability to see plans unfolding. For her, the Red Moon is more a power of civilization, fear, and coercion than an inclusive mystery.

She tried to integrate the Moon Rune and even had it tattooed on her forehead. But she is not yet ready to be fully initiated into the Lunar Way.

### What this Portrait shows

Hanya is not "a Lunar warrior" accompanied by a list of abilities. Her religion, Runes, and personality do not overlap perfectly.

Her magic itself cannot be separated from how she understands Hwarin: it comes from a teaching, a relationship that is still incomplete, and a set of divine gestures she is learning to recognize.

That gap is precisely what makes her interesting to play.

## Peek-ee-peek

> “The prophecy is coming true and our tribe was chosen long ago to be the protectors of the Moon.”

![Peek](/img/narratives/the-lunar-way/heroes/peek-ee-peek/peek-drawing-600x.png)

**Woman, 22 rains — Praxian Animist, daughter of a Khan.**

Peek-ee-peek is a Sand Nomad scout. She carries a bow, lance, helmet, and fetishes, but the most important presence beside her is **Fta-Ah**, her sable antelope.

### Runes

![](/img/runes/moon.png) **Moon** — sense of superiority, curiosity, prophetic conviction.

![](/img/runes/death.png) **Death** — sure arrow, sharp opinions, impulsiveness.

![](/img/runes/spirit.png) **Spirits** — sensitivity to the unseen world, seeing and feeling spirits, bargaining with them.

### Sand Nomad

- be one with her antelope;
- despise horses and sedentary people;
- survive in the Wastes;
- navigate;
- know the customs, myths, and geography of Prax;
- read Praxian knots;
- **relationships:** her tribe, her father Paak-the-Cunning;
- **virtues:** pride, freedom.

### Tradition of Waha the Butcher

- **virtues:** eliminate Chaos, soothe the soul of a slain animal, feed, defend, and warn the tribe;
- **miracle:** Death Lance.

Peek carries several fetishes. Each gives her a power but also imposes a way of living:

- **Kill the Stranger** — do not kill for one moon afterwards;
- **Know the Lineage** — sacrifice to the ancestors at every full moon;
- **Beast Above Man** — be vegetarian;
- **Water-Seeking Spirit** — drink nothing but water;
- **Pacification of Spirits** — never defile a place inhabited by a spirit.

#### Tradition story — Waha's Tasks

In the Darkness, Waha appears in a ravaged world where the survivors are scattered and dying. He gathers them and teaches them new ways to live. He frees Eiritha's Protectresses so the herds may return, teaches how Death can serve life, fights or tames dangerous powers, and cleanses the Wastelands of the Devil's presence. His Tasks eventually bind together again the peoples, spirits, and different parts of Prax.

This story explains why Peek's Tradition does not easily separate **survival**, **Death**, **struggle against Chaos**, **protection of the herd**, and **duty to the tribe**.

But Peek is an animist: she does not turn this story into a list of miracles to reproduce. Her magic passes through the **concrete relationships with spirits** she carries in her fetishes. Waha's Tasks teach her which relationships matter and what world those spirits help maintain; the taboos say what those relationships require of her.

### Fta-Ah

**Sable antelope — courageous, fast, faithful.**

Fta-Ah is not filed under "equipment": the relationship deserves its own Life Chapter.

### What this Portrait shows

For Peek, the character's powers cannot be separated from her relationships, obligations, and way of inhabiting Prax.

A fetish is not merely something she owns. It asks something of her in return. A tradition story is no more a list of powers: it gives meaning to the relationships that make her magic possible.

## Talimar, called Tal

> “Things are. Even when contrary, they are there, together.”

**Man, 20 — Sartarite of the Grey Heron clan, newly initiated into Lhankor Mhy.**

Tal shows another property of the Living Portrait: it can **transform because the Protagonist comes to understand what they lived differently**.

### ![](/img/runes/air.png) Air — the middle

Tal recognizes himself in the space between things: earth and sky, high and low, clouds, wind, Moon and stars. Far from his clan, Air has also become the tangible reminder of his roots.

### ![](/img/runes/law.png) Law — what binds

Tal first called **Harmony** his intuition that different, even contrary things could belong to the same Cosmos.

During his initiation, he understands this experience differently: for him, it was more deeply **Law**, what binds without erasing differences.

### ![](/img/runes/truth.png) Truth — what sets him in motion

Tal first called **Movement** his curiosity and desire to go elsewhere.

His initiation reveals a direction: that movement was seeking **Truth**. Not certainty possessed, but what compels him to keep searching.

### Young Grey Sage

- insatiable curiosity;
- distinguishes what he knows from what he thinks he knows;
- sustains several hypotheses without prematurely turning one into a truth;
- travelled in Tarsh with his uncle Grimar;
- fascinated and troubled by Lunar civilization;
- tablet of the seven differences given by Hendrik;
- ancestral knife given by his father.

### Myth of Lhankor Mhy — the quest for knowledge

Hendrik told Tal the myth of **Lhankor Mhy**, born of **Orenoar, Mistress of Truth**, upon **Mostal, the Mountain of Law**. The traditions themselves do not all agree on his father. When knowledge is lost in the Darkness and Elasa disappears, Lhankor Mhy invents writing to preserve what has been learned, then continues his quest with the Lightbringers.

Hendrik stresses one point that marks Tal deeply: even Lhankor Mhy, who embraces all knowledge, **sometimes succeeds and sometimes fails**. Yet he continues to seek.

Tal retains five gestures from the myth:

- distinguish what is true;
- remember what has been learned;
- preserve knowledge through writing;
- seek what is missing;
- continue searching even when one already knows.

Before his initiation, Tal already knew this myth, meditated on it, and had begun to recognize his own way of acting within it. Initiation changes the nature of that knowledge: he is no longer merely someone who understands a story about Lhankor Mhy; he is **connected to the God**.

These five formulations are therefore not five spells. They are five gestures of the God that Tal has learned to recognize and can now seek to extend within a Situation. **The myth gives him the keys; initiation opens the relationship that makes them operative.**

### Bonds that matter

- **Calimar and Urestona**, his parents;
- **Grimar**, his merchant uncle;
- **Hendrik, son of Enderos**, his teacher;
- **the Grey Heron clan**, his roots.

### Trace — initiation

Tal's initiation did not erase what had previously constituted him. It gave it another meaning.

It also transformed his relationship to the myth he learned from Hendrik: what had been teaching, reflection, and possibility now becomes a path through which Tal can seek to echo the gestures of Lhankor Mhy.

His Portrait therefore changes without a retcon: **the character changed because his understanding of himself and his relationship to the Cosmos changed.**

## Portraits, not templates

Hanya, Peek, and Tal do not have the same Portrait architecture.

That is intentional.

The Portrait should make visible **what constitutes this particular person**, not prove that every Protagonist possesses the same categories.

Someone encountered for only a few minutes may still fit into one intention and two striking traits. If they return and become important, their Portrait can grow exactly like that of a lasting Protagonist.
