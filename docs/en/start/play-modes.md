# Organizing Play

Glorantha Perspectives does not require a single distribution of roles. A way of playing simply states **who carries the Protagonists and how Time is carried**.

## One person mainly carries Time

Each player may carry one or more Protagonists, while another person mainly carries Time: they present the Situation, act for the world, and settle the points that need a decision.

This is the arrangement closest to traditional roleplaying with a GM and player characters.

## Time is shared

Players may divide or rotate the responsibilities of Time. One presents a Situation, another acts for a Force, and another settles a point of coherence when the table gives them that responsibility.

The distribution may remain stable for a whole scene or change when the fiction makes it useful.

## One person carries both the Protagonists and Time

In solo play, the same person carries the Protagonists and Time. Runes, oracles, tables, Glorantha sources, or an AI can help produce what the player does not want to decide for themselves.

The goal is not to simulate a second player, but to preserve genuine room for discovery and uncertainty.

## Other arrangements are possible

You can distribute these responsibilities differently: rotating Time, shared Protagonists, correspondence play, digital assistance, campaigns where responsibility for Time changes with place or era...

As long as the table knows **who can decide what at that moment**, the rules do not need to be rebuilt.
