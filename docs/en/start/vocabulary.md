# Protagonists and Time

A few words are enough to make clear who does what around the table.

## Player

Anyone taking part in the game is a **player**. Depending on how the game is organized, a player may carry a Protagonist, part of Time, several of these responsibilities, or alternate between them.

## Protagonist

A **Protagonist** is a person, group, or other entity whose intentions, choices, and transformations the game follows.

Carrying a Protagonist means deciding what they want, what they attempt, what they say, and how they react to what they experience.

## Time

**Time** is the game function that keeps Glorantha alive around the Protagonists.

Carrying Time includes, among other things:

- presenting what the Protagonists can perceive;
- acting for people, groups, and powers that are not carried as Protagonists;
- advancing what continues to act without them;
- preserving the coherence of the world;
- responding to the Protagonists' actions;
- introducing the unexpected when the Situation calls for it.

Time is not there to defeat the Protagonists, protect a plot, or decide their future.

**Time makes the world live. The Protagonists choose what they do with it.**

### Why Time?

In Glorantha, the Great Compromise gives Time a special place. Divine powers are bound to the order they accepted, while mortals live within Time and can still choose, act, and change the world.

The term therefore points to an essential distinction: the framework exists, but the Protagonists' destiny is not written for them.
