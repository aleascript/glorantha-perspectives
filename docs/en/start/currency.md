# Currency and Exchange

<div data-runes="chapter">
  <img src="/img/runes/exchange.png" alt="Exchange" />
</div>

Glorantha is a world of communities. Exchange therefore often says something about those who guarantee it: clan, temple, city, cult, empire, or merchant network.

You do not need detailed accounting to play. The main thing to remember is that a currency has value because people and institutions recognize it.

## High-value currencies

- **Guilders**: bronze currency associated with Issaries and major trade;
- **Lunars**: silver currency of the Lunar Empire;
- **Wheels**: large Dara Happan gold coins, especially rare and prestigious;
- **Bolgs**: pierced pieces of lead used among the Uz.

Exact usage varies by region and culture. A currency recognized in one place may be awkward, suspicious, or difficult to exchange elsewhere.

## Small coinage and barter

Most ordinary transactions may use barter or lower-value units:

- copper coins;
- aluminum stars in the Lunar Empire;
- clacks among the Uz;
- goods directly useful to the community.

The exact price matters less than the relationship: who accepts this exchange, who guarantees the value, who incurs a debt, and what does this transaction mean here?

## In play

When wealth, debt, cargo, or the scarcity of a currency becomes important in the Situation, record it as a concrete fictional element. It may then become a Bet, a resource that can be lost, an obligation, or a problem to solve.

Otherwise, simply let exchange remain part of the world without turning it into an economic subsystem.
