# Currency and Exchange

![Exchange](/img/runes/exchange.png)

Glorantha is a world of communities. Barter and small coinage play a major role in everyday life, while high-value currencies are often tied to temples, cities, and authorities that guarantee exchange.

## High-value currencies

- **Guilders**: bronze currency associated with Issaries and major trade;
- **Lunars**: silver currency of the Lunar Empire;
- **Wheels**: large Dara Happan gold coins, especially rare and prestigious;
- **Bolgs**: pierced pieces of lead used among the Uz.

Exact usage varies by region and culture. A currency recognized in one place may be awkward, suspicious, or difficult to exchange elsewhere.

## Small coinage and barter

Most ordinary transactions may use barter or lower-value units:

- copper coins;
- aluminum stars in the Lunar Empire;
- clacks among the Uz;
- goods directly useful to the community.

## In play

Money does not necessarily require detailed accounting.

When wealth, debt, cargo, or the scarcity of a currency becomes important in the Situation, record it as a concrete truth. It may then become a Bet, a resource that can be lost, an obligation, or a problem to solve.
