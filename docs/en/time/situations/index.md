# Situations

A **Situation** is a local world already in motion before the Protagonists act.

Time does not prepare what the Protagonists will do or the story that must happen. It prepares enough truths, relationships, and tensions to answer their choices honestly.

A useful Situation makes a few simple elements visible:

- **Forces** that want something and can act;
- **Pressures** that keep developing if no one intervenes;
- what is likely to happen **if the Protagonists do nothing**;
- truths of the world that may become relevant depending on Intentions and Foci;
- optionally one or more **Promises**: images, revelations, or moments that Time hopes to bring to life if the fiction naturally opens the way.

A Promise is not a scene to reach. It belongs to the world and does not depend on one particular Protagonist. If the table's choices never lead to it, it may remain unseen.

There is no need to prepare the Bets for every possible resolution. When something becomes both important and genuinely uncertain, the table states the Intention, makes consequences visible, chooses the Focus and Zoom, then looks at **what matters here and now**.

> **Prepare what exists and what is pushing. Do not prepare what the Protagonists will do.**
