# The Imprint of the Runes

In Glorantha, Runes are not only abstract symbols. They can physically mark people, objects, buildings, and landscapes.

## Runic colors

A strong affinity may appear in skin, eyes, hair, clothing, materials, or magical effects.

Some useful associations:

- **Air**: orange or blue;
- **Earth**: olive green or brown;
- **Moon**: red or white;
- **Water**: blue or white;
- **Darkness**: dull gray or black;
- **Fire/Sky**: yellow or red.

These marks may be present from birth, appear at initiation, intensify during a ceremony, or change after a Heroquest.

## Runic forms

Forms themselves may echo the Runes: architecture, jewelry, weapons, fields, coins, clothing patterns, or ritual objects.

There is no universal rule imposing the same shape on every culture. Instead ask how **this culture** makes **this Rune** visible.

## During a Heroquest

In the Otherworld, runic correspondences may become much clearer than in everyday life. A color can saturate an entire scene, a body can draw closer to a mythical form, or an object can suddenly reveal what it represents.

A significant enough transformation can return with the Protagonist and become a new lasting truth.

## In play

The runic imprint mainly serves to make the world visible.

When it becomes important in a Situation, it can also become a Stake: a color recognized as the sign of a cult, a weapon whose form expresses Death, a bodily mark that reveals an initiation, and so on.
