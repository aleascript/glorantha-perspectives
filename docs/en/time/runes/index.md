# Runes

**Runes** are the fundamental forms through which Glorantha can be read and acted upon.

They can appear in a Protagonist, place, relationship, cult, object, event, or Situation. A Rune can become a **Stake** when it genuinely matters to what is happening; it never grants an automatic advantage.

## In play

Runes can, among other things:

- describe what is true of a Protagonist;
- give shape to magic, an obligation, or a relationship;
- reveal what matters in a Situation;
- support a Stake when the fiction allows it;
- provide inspiration when Time needs something to emerge.

To use Runes as a tool for exploration and inspiration, see [Runic Inspiration](../../time/runic-inspiration/).
