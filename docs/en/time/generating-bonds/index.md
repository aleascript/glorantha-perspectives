# Generating a Bond

When Time needs to quickly imagine a geas, taboo, or prohibition, a Power Rune can provide a starting point.

The Rune does not give a mandatory answer. It narrows the field of possibilities and helps formulate a bond that fits the spirit, cult, place, or Situation.

## Check the bond before introducing it

Ask four questions:

1. **Why does this bond exist here?**
2. **Who or what imposes it?**
3. **What does it grant or protect?**
4. **What fictional consequence would follow from breaking it?**

If the answers do not arise naturally from Glorantha and the Situation, keep the Rune as inspiration and look for another formulation.
