# Talimar's Road

**Talimar's Road** comes from an actual game of Glorantha Perspectives. Its first session let the Protagonist emerge through play rather than beginning with a completed character sheet.

This narrative is not a mandatory creation method. It shows one simple possibility: **begin with a few truths, play, then let the character become more precise as the fiction gives them a history.**

## Read Talimar's Road

Three views let you go further:

- [**Talimar's character sheet**](character.md) — the Protagonist as he stands at the end of the first session;
- [**Actual Play — first session**](actual-play.md) — a detailed account of choices, Bets, rolls, and table decisions;
- [**Story — becoming a Grey Sage**](story.md) — the same events told as a continuous narrative.

## Tal before the sheet

Talimar, called **Tal**, belongs to the Grey Heron clan of the Colymar tribe.

He is one of his people, but their way of telling the world no longer entirely satisfies him. He cannot condemn the Lunar Empire as a single block: he has seen its roads, buildings, baths, organization, and ways of looking at conflict that fascinated him. He also knows that the same civilization practices domination and slavery, and that the Crimson Bat exists beneath the same Moon.

This contradiction is not a trait selected to complete a sheet. It appears in the conversation of play and gradually becomes one of Tal's central truths.

## His uncle's roads

A merchant uncle often took him into Tarsh when he was younger. His family could naturally imagine that he would follow the same road and move toward Issaries.

But trade interests him very little. It is the roads themselves that draw him: the different stories told from place to place, old stelae, genealogies, myths that contradict one another, and behind them a question that keeps returning:

> **How can we know what is true?**

That is how **Lhankor Mhy** becomes a possibility. Not as a career selected from a list, but as a possible answer to something the game has just discovered about Tal.

## Playing the path to initiation

The player then chooses not to decide immediately who recruited Tal, or even whether he will be initiated. He would rather **play that meeting and that possibility through to their conclusion**.

The fiction brings him to **Hendrik**, a Grey Sage of Lhankor Mhy. Their exchanges, teachings, myths, and Situations gradually give Tal reasons to enter that path.

Creation therefore continues during play: relationships, questions, experiences, and understandings become lasting truths that may later become Bets.

## Runes understood differently

At one point in his path, Tal understands himself through **Air**, **Harmony**, and **Movement**.

Initiation does not reveal that these Runes were “wrong”. It lets him see more precisely what he had been naming through them.

For him:

- what he called **Harmony** was an intuition of **Law**: what binds without erasing differences;
- what he called **Movement** expressed, at a deeper level, **Truth**: what pushes him to keep searching even when an answer already seems available.

His description becomes **Air, Law, Truth**.

The sheet changes because Tal's understanding has changed. This is neither a retroactive correction nor character optimization: **the change to the sheet is itself an event in the fiction.**

## What Talimar's Road shows

That first session followed a very simple loop:

> **lived fiction → truths about the Protagonist → new possible Bets → new fiction**

A Protagonist therefore does not always need to be known in every detail before play begins.

You can start with enough solid elements to play, then add, transform, or remove truths as Situations reveal who the Protagonist has become.

This is one way to create a character with Glorantha Perspectives. It is not the only one.
