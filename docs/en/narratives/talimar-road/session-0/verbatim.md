## Tal's training

**[PJ]**

I think at some point it would be good to present the myths of Lhankor Mhy, then deal with initiation. At this stage there's also the question of Runes. I think things are clear now, what are LM's Runes?

**[MJ]**

Yes. And now that Tal is beginning to exist, the Runes become interesting because we can **recognize them in him**, rather than assign them abstractly.

For **Lhankor Mhy**, the two fundamental Runes that stand out from the sources are **Truth** and **Law**. *Storm Tribe* has him born of **Orenoar, Mistress of Truth**, upon **Mostal, the Mountain of Law**; there he discerns and states cosmic Law. The same book explicitly calls him the god of “Truth, Knowledge, and Law.” Later HeroQuest formulations translate this into the affinities **Knowledge, Law, Literacy**.

And that makes our Tal rather interesting.

**Truth**, for him, already seems almost obvious to me. Not “I possess the truth,” precisely. More this inability to be satisfied by an answer because it is comfortable. The Orlanthi/Lunar contrast threw him into crisis because he discovered that what he had been told was no longer enough. And before the stone, he was immediately absorbed in: *what is actually there?*

**Law** is more surprising — and potentially more interesting. Tal does not seem at all in love with social law, tradition, or established order. But Lhankor Mhy's Law is deeper: the idea that amid the noise of the world, **there are relationships that can be understood, distinguished, named**. That is precisely what calms him before the stone and the tablet.

So I especially do not want to conclude too quickly: “apprentice of Lhankor Mhy = Tal receives Truth + Law.” **His god possesses these Runes; Tal still has to show how he resonates with them.**

A few years of apprenticeship can now let us discover three things: **Tal's own Runes**, his growing understanding of **Lhankor Mhy's myths**, then the moment when Hendrik decides — or not — that he can attempt initiation.

I would even suggest not choosing the Runes now. Hendrik has several apprentices. We play **2 or 3 very elliptical Situations from his training**, each posing a different problem for Tal. His answers should let his runic affinities emerge naturally.

Then Hendrik teaches him the myths that give meaning to what he has just lived: **the birth upon the Mountain of Law; the loss of Elasa and the Light of Knowledge; the invention of writing; Lhankor Mhy among the Lightbringers; his successes but also his failures during the Quest**. *Storm Tribe* also insists on an idea I especially like for Tal: Lhankor Mhy embraces all knowledge, but **mortals never can; their knowledge necessarily remains incomplete**.

And only then: **initiation**.

I think that one, unlike the years of apprenticeship, **probably deserves to be played**.
