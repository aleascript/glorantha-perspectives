# Talimar's Character Sheet

This sheet represents **Talimar after his initiation into Lhankor Mhy**, at the end of the first session of Talimar's Road. It preserves what play has brought into view about him rather than a state prepared before play.

## Identity

**Name:** Talimar, called Tal  
**Age:** 20  
**Quote:** “Things are. Even when contrary, they are there, together.”

## Origins and community

- **Culture:** Sartarite / Heortling
- **Clan:** Grey Heron
- **Tribe:** Colymar
- **Parents:** Calimar and Urestona, carls of the clan

## Worldview

**Theist — initiate of Lhankor Mhy.**

Tal apprenticed under **Hendrik, son of Enderos**, a Grey Sage of Lhankor Mhy, before undergoing his initiation.

## Runes

### Air — the middle

![Air](/img/runes/air.png)

Tal recognizes himself in the space between things: earth and sky, high and low, clouds, wind, Moon and stars. Far from his clan, Air has also become the tangible reminder of his roots.

### Law — what binds

![Law](/img/runes/law.png)

Tal first recognized this aspiration as **Harmony**: things can be different, even contrary, while still belonging to the same Cosmos. During initiation, he understands that what he was trying to name was, for him, **Law**: what binds things without erasing their differences.

### Truth — what sets him in motion

![Truth](/img/runes/truth.png)

Tal first recognized this aspiration as **Movement**, his curiosity and need to go elsewhere. During initiation, he understands that this movement had a direction: he was seeking **Truth**. Truth is not certainty possessed, but what compels him to continue searching.

## Keywords and traits

- Insatiable curiosity
- Young Grey Sage / new initiate of Lhankor Mhy
- Distinguishes what he knows from what he thinks he knows
- Can sustain several hypotheses without prematurely turning one into truth
- Traveled in Tarsh with his uncle Grimar
- Fascinated and troubled by Lunar civilization
- Tablet of the seven differences, kept since his meeting with Hendrik
- Ancestral knife given by his father

## Known myth — Lhankor Mhy and the quest for knowledge

What Tal has personally taken from it:

- distinguish what is true;
- remember what has been learned;
- preserve knowledge through writing;
- seek what is missing;
- continue searching even when one already knows.

These phrases currently express Tal's personal understanding of the myth. Their magical reach remains to be discovered in play.

## Bonds and attachments

- **Calimar and Urestona** — his parents, carls of the Grey Heron.
- **Grimar** — his merchant uncle; deep affection despite the professional road Tal did not follow.
- **Hendrik** — his teacher, who taught him to seek rather than recite.
- **Grey Heron clan** — his roots, paradoxically felt more strongly since leaving them.
- **The old Grey Sage of the clan** — traditional and repetitive in young Tal's eyes; his presence nevertheless revealed the existence of Lhankor Mhy to Tal early on.

## State

No particular state at present.

## Latest event

Tal has just undergone his initiation into Lhankor Mhy. In the experience accompanying his acceptance, **Law** and **Truth** give new meaning to what he previously called Harmony and Movement.

He felt as though he stood in the Gods World and heard again the question that began his road:

> **“Tell me what is wrong.”**
