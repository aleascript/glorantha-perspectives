# The Three Stones Source

Peek-ee-Peek reaches a spring in the Wastes just as a Lunar team is preparing a water relay for the imperial road. Work is due to begin at dawn; to dig the cistern, the surveyor intends to move one of the stones guarding the spring.

It is the night of the full moon, and Peek still has to perform her sacrifice to the ancestors.

This session was played with the current Glorantha Perspectives rules from a very short Situation, also published as a [**Time preparation example**](../../../time/situations/three-stones-source.md).

## Two ways to read the session

- [**Verbatim**](verbatim.md) — the session in its actual order: fiction, Intentions, Foci, Bets, rolls, and table corrections.
- [**Backstage of Time**](backstage.md) — the Situation prepared before play: Forces, Pressures, what happens if no one acts, and the Promise.

There is deliberately **no separate Story, Debrief, or new Portrait** for this session. The verbatim record already carries the story of play; the Backstage lets readers compare what was prepared with what emerged; Peek's [existing portrait](../../the-lunar-way/heroes/peek-ee-peek/) remains the reference until the fiction durably changes what constitutes her.
