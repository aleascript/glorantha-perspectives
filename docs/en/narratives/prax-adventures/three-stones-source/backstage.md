# Backstage of Time

This page reproduces the session preparation before Peek-ee-Peek acted.

## Forces

- **Ostar Varenis**, surveyor from the garrison of **Moonbroth**. He wants a water relay for the imperial road. He is not evil: he does not perceive the spirit and sees an inconvenient stone where Peek sees a presence.
- **Hirrak and the Sable escort.** They want to honour Paak's word and bring back the promised money. Hirrak loves his sister, but he gave his word before the Khan.
- **Mother-of-Reeds.** She tolerates drinking, but not touching the stones. If the stone is moved, she will leave or take revenge.
- **The other tribes.** The spring is shared under Praxian custom. A guarded Lunar cistern would change who may drink there.

## Pressures

- Work begins at dawn.
- It is the full moon, and the sacrifice to the ancestors cannot wait.
- The animals already refuse to drink.

## If Peek does nothing

The porters move the stone in the morning. The spring turns foul for days, and a neighbouring tribe accuses the Sables of defiling a spirit-place.

## What may matter if Peek acts

Without assuming anything about her Intention:

- Her taboo **"never defile a place inhabited by a spirit"** may become a Frame Factor: she cannot take part in the works, whatever her father says.
- Many Bets may become relevant: **Khan's Daughter**, **Bargain with spirits**, **Customs of Prax**, **Sense of superiority** — which may also count against her with Ostar —, **Strong opinions**, **Impulsive**.
- On the other side: **Paak's word given to the Empire**, the armed escort, and Hirrak himself.
- Her Worldview is **Animism**. That night, if her relationship to the Moon truly matters — her tribe believes it was "chosen to protect the Moon" — the **Full Moon Lunar Influence** applies outside the Glowline: two `1`s forming a spiritual Pact count as **2 Successes**.

## Promise

Under the full moon, tradition says that Mother-of-Reeds shows **two moons in the water, the red one and a white one**. No one knows what the second means.

This Promise **belongs to the world, not to Peek**. The moment would remain remarkable with any other Protagonist present.
