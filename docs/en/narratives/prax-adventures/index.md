# Adventures in Prax

Alongside [**The Lunar Way**](../the-lunar-way/), these sessions follow **Peek-ee-Peek** through new Situations in Prax using the current Glorantha Perspectives rules.

Peek remains the character born from *The Lunar Way*. These adventures are not an eighteenth chapter of that older campaign, however: they form an autonomous series centred on what she now experiences in her own land.

Her existing [**portrait**](../the-lunar-way/heroes/peek-ee-peek/) remains the reference portrait. It is updated only when the fiction genuinely changes what constitutes Peek; the ordinary events of her adventures belong first to her history.

## Sessions

- [**The Three Stones Source**](three-stones-source/) — a Praxian spring, a Lunar worksite, Paak's word, and a night of the full moon.

When the verbatim play record is already strong enough to carry the story, **Adventures in Prax** deliberately keeps the form actually played instead of automatically producing a literary retelling.
