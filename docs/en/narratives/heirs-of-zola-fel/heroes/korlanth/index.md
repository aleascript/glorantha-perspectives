*Son of Orlanthi immigrants who came to Pavis after the defeat against the Lunar Empire, Korlanth is a jack-of-all-trades (building sites, odd jobs, marauding...). He trains with weapons with his friends and follows the teachings of the Secret Wind, which he has never betrayed. He believes in his Destiny.*
