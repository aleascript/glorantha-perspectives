# Time's Backstage

:::warning[Spoilers]

This page reveals the preparation for the game. It contains no Debrief: the elements below are those that existed before Peek acted.

:::

## Forces

- **Ostar Varenis**, surveyor from the Moonbroth garrison. He wants a water station for the Imperial road. He is not malicious: he does not perceive the spirit and sees an inconvenient stone where Peek sees a presence.
- **Hirrak and the Sable escort.** They want to honor Paak's word and bring home the promised money. Hirrak loves his sister, but he gave his word before the Khan.
- **Mother-of-Reeds.** She tolerates drinking, but not touching the stones. If the stone is moved, she will leave or take revenge.
- **The other tribes.** The spring is shared under Praxian custom. A guarded Lunar cistern would change who may drink there.

## Pressures

- Work begins at dawn.
- It is the full moon, and the sacrifice to the ancestors cannot wait.
- The animals already refuse to drink.

**If Peek does nothing**, the porters move the stone in the morning. The spring turns foul for days, and a neighboring tribe accuses the Sables of defiling a spirit place.

## What may matter if Peek acts

Without presupposing her Intention:

- Her taboo, "never defile a place inhabited by a spirit," may become a Frame Factor: she cannot take part in the works, whatever her father says.
- Many Bets are possible: *Daughter of the Khan*, *Bargain with spirits*, *Customs of Prax*, *Sense of superiority* — which may also count against her with Ostar —, *Strong opinions*, *Impulsive*.
- Opposite her stand Paak's word to the Empire, the armed escort, and Hirrak himself.
- Her Worldview is **Animism**. And that night, if her relationship to the Moon truly matters — her tribe believes itself "chosen to protect the Moon" —, the **Full Moon Lunar Influence** applies outside the Glowline: two 1s forming a Spirit Pact are worth 2 Successes.

## Promise

**This Promise belongs to the world, not to Peek.** Under the full moon, tradition says Mother-of-Reeds shows two moons in the water, the red and a white one. No one knows what the second means.

The moment would remain remarkable with any other Protagonist present.
