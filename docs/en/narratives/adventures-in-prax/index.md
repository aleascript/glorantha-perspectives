# Adventures in Prax

Situations played in Prax with the current Glorantha Perspectives rules.

Each story follows one or more Protagonists, sometimes drawn from other chronicles. The stories stand on their own: you don't need to know those chronicles to read them.

When a **Verbatim** is enough to tell the story, it is published in its play form rather than systematically rewritten as prose. Likewise, not every game receives a Debrief or a new Portrait: those documents exist only when they genuinely add something.

- [**The Three Stones Spring**](three-stones-source/) — with Peek-ee-Peek: a Praxian spring, a Lunar worksite and a full-moon night.
