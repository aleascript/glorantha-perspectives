# Session 0 — Breath and Lightning

**Played as a true Session 0**, this first published session is both **Edkored's played character creation** and the **entry point of his chronicle**.

The session begins with the character's emergence: his voice, Runes, life chapters, worldview, and the kind of Orlanthi he has become after the Lunar ban on Orlanth worship.

It then shifts into a full first Situation. Sorane warns Edkored that a Tarshite patrol is threatening an old Kolati shaman. After drawing the soldiers away with Hedkoranth's lightning, Edkored follows a sign from Orlanth to Still Lake. There he finds Vorlak, a badly wounded young Mudhen, and refuses to let him die.

The session ends in a boat, travelling toward Orstalor Spearlord's hidden crannog.
