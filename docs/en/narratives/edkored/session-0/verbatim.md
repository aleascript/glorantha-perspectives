# Transcript

This page preserves the game in its real order: Edkored's and Time's words, Focuses, Bets, dice, and escalation. This English version is a faithful translation of the French play transcript.

---

**[TIME]**

A shadow detaches itself from the brush. You recognize Sorane, a young Yinkini warrior.

“Edkored, you were making too much noise with the lightning. A patrol of Tarshite light infantry is coming up the western ravine with a hound. If they keep going, they'll stumble onto the old Kolati shaman within fifteen minutes. There are six of them, well armed. What do we do?”

**[EDKORED]**

“Sorane, always there to bring me grim news. But we have to act, and fast.”

My plan is to set off a thunderstone on the far side of the cave to draw them away. Sorane will make another diversion farther off with her alynxes. We know Dogbone well enough to lose them.

## Resolution 1 — The Diversion

- **Focus:** make the patrol lose the shaman's trail and escape.
- **Edkored — Theism:** 4 Bets — Air Rune, Hill Survivor, Sorane, Movement Rune.
- **Patrol — Theism under the Half Moon:** 3 Bets — Experienced Infantry, Hound, Discipline.
- **Edkored roll:** 6, 6, 3, 3 → reroll odds: 4, 1 → **3 Successes**.
- **Patrol roll:** 2, 5, 6 → reroll the odd: 3 → **2 Successes**.
- **Result: Edkored Victory.**

**[TIME]**

Hedkoranth's lightning shatters an oak. The patrol turns around. Sorane and her alynxes sow confusion. You escape and the shaman is safe, but the hunt has begun.

Later, a raven of Orlanth approaches.

**[EDKORED]**

“Greetings, messenger of the Gods.”

I try to see whether it carries a message.

**[TIME]**

It drops a small pierced alynx fang and flies northeast, toward Torkan Valley and Still Lake.

**[EDKORED]**

I take up the divine message. I set off at a run toward Torkan Valley.

**[TIME]**

When you reach the edge of Still Lake in the night and cold, you find a wounded shadowcat and a young man collapsed in the mud. He bears the insignia of the Mudhens, Orstalor Spearlord's rebels. Out on the lake, a red light flashes: a signal.

**[EDKORED]**

I approach carefully with the fetish.

“Peace.”

I want to make a mud poultice to stabilize them urgently, in ten minutes.

“Earth and water, keep the breath of life within him.”

## Resolution 2 — First Aid

- **Focus:** stabilize the wounds with what is available.
- **Edkored — Theism:** 3 Bets — Known Ritual, Mud/water present, Alynx's Acceptance.
- **Adversity — Middle World:** 3 Bets — Depth of the Wound, Cold/damp, Urgency/exposure.
- **Edkored roll:** 5, 2, 2 → **2 Successes**.
- **Adversity roll:** 2, 3, 6 → **2 Successes**.
- **Result: Status quo.** The boy does not die, but the situation remains precarious and the distant light is moving away.

**[EDKORED]**

Escalation! I refuse to give up. I implore the gods: this is the Bet of desperation.

- **Edkored roll:** 6, 5, 4, 1 → reroll: 2 → **3 Successes**.
- **Adversity roll:** +1 Bet for the cold; 4, 3, 1, 1 → **1 Success**.
- **Result: Feat!**

**[TIME]**

Orlanth's magic crackles. The bleeding stops dead. The sparks draw a Mudhen boat toward you. Three men approach, wary, spears in hand. Their leader gives the Heortling ritual challenge:

“Halt, stranger! Who comes here?”

**[EDKORED]**

Hands raised, displaying my Runes:

“I am Edkored. Orlanth guided me here... I found a young Mudhen wounded. Hurry, he needs treatment.”

**[TIME]**

The warrior, Gringolf, lowers his spear and offers you hospitality. He invites you aboard to reach the secret crannog.

**[EDKORED]**

I climb aboard in silence, darkly aware of the Empire, but anchored in the resilience of my Heortling ancestors.
