# Edkored

**Edkored** is an actual game of Glorantha Perspectives, published as a playtest chronicle set in Sartar.

The chronicle begins with an already established Protagonist: **Edkored**, 30 years old, an Orlanthi theist, solitary adept of Orlanth, and fighter of the **Wind Maquis**. Air, Movement, and Death already shape his outlook when play begins.

Unlike *Talimar's Road*, this Session 0 therefore does not play through character creation. The number simply marks the **entry point of this new chronicle**.

## Reading a session

Each session is preserved in four complementary forms:

- **Transcript** — the game in its real order, including exchanges, Focuses, Bets, and dice;
- **Story** — the same session told as a continuous story;
- **Time Backstage** — what the played session lets us analyse about the Situation and resolutions, without exposing threads that remain active;
- **Living Portrait** — Edkored's state exactly at the end of the session.

Living Portraits remain **historical snapshots**: later sessions will have their own Portraits.

:::warning[Backstage]

**Time Backstage** pages do not publish information that remains secret for the campaign.

:::

## Sessions

### [Session 0](session-0/index.md) — Breath and Lightning

In the Dogbone hills, Edkored diverts a Tarshite patrol threatening a Kolati shaman. A sign from Orlanth then draws him toward Still Lake, where he barely saves Vorlak, a young Mudhen rebel, before boarding a boat bound for Orstalor Spearlord's crannog.
