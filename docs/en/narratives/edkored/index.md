# Edkored

**Edkored** is an actual game of Glorantha Perspectives, published as a playtest chronicle set in Sartar.

The chronicle begins with a **Session 0 that serves both as played character creation and as a true opening adventure**. Edkored does not enter play as an already finished sheet: his voice, Runes, life chapters, and first Situation all emerge within the same continuity of play.

He thus appears as **an Orlanthi man in his thirties**, a theist, a solitary adept of Orlanth who has taken to the outlaw life in the Dogbone hills. **Air, Movement, and Death** already structure what he recognizes in himself when the Living Portrait takes shape.

## Reading a session

Each session is preserved in four complementary forms:

- **Transcript** — the game in its real order, including exchanges, Focuses, Bets, and dice;
- **Story** — the same session told as a continuous story;
- **Time Backstage** — what the played session lets us analyse about the Situation and resolutions, without exposing threads that remain active;
- **Living Portrait** — Edkored's state exactly at the end of the session.

Living Portraits remain **historical snapshots**: later sessions will have their own Portraits.

:::warning[Backstage]

**Time Backstage** pages do not publish information that remains secret for the campaign.

:::

## Sessions

### [Session 0](session-0/index.md) — Breath and Lightning

This session first plays through **the character's emergence**: concept, voice, Runes, life chapters, and worldview.

It then shifts into a full first Situation. In the Dogbone hills, Edkored diverts a Tarshite patrol threatening a Kolati shaman. A sign from Orlanth then draws him toward Still Lake, where he barely saves Vorlak, a young Mudhen rebel, before boarding a boat bound for Orstalor Spearlord's crannog.

This “Session 0” therefore fully serves its double function: **bringing the Protagonist into existence and immediately setting his story in motion**.
