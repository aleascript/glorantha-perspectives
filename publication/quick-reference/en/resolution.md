# Resolution

Resolve when a Situation contains something **genuinely important and genuinely uncertain**.

## Grand Cycle

| Step | Rune | Procedure |
| ---: | --- | --- |
| 1 | ![Movement](/img/runes/movement.png) **Movement** | State the **Intentions**. |
| 2 | ![Death](/img/runes/death.png) **Death** | Make visible the **important consequences** the Protagonists can reasonably know. |
| 3 | ![Harmony](/img/runes/harmony.png) **Harmony** | Set the **Focus**, **Zoom**, and **two outcomes** in the balance. |
| 4 | ![Stasis](/img/runes/stasis.png) **Stasis** | Identify **Frame Factors**, then gather the **Bets** on each side. |
| 5 | ![Life](/img/runes/life.png) **Life** | Roll **one die per Bet**. |
| 6 | ![Disorder](/img/runes/disorder.png) **Disorder** | Read each roll according to the applicable **Worldview**. |
| 7 | ![Truth](/img/runes/truth.png) **Truth** | Compare Successes and determine the outcome. |
| 8 | ![Illusion](/img/runes/illusion.png) **Illusion** | Give the result fictional form and continue from the transformed Situation. |

**Situation → Intention → consequences → Focus / Zoom → two outcomes → Frame Factors / Bets → roll → Worldview → comparison → manifestation → transformed Situation**

## 1 — Movement: Intention

An **Intention** is what a Protagonist, Force, or other actor seeks to obtain.

It gives the Situation a direction. It is not yet the Focus.

## 2 — Death: consequences

Before the roll, state significant consequences the Protagonist can reasonably know.

The player may then keep the Intention, change method, or reduce the objective.

Possible warnings:

- **“This could cost you dearly.”**
- **“Failure could be fatal.”**
- **“Chaos is watching.”**

## 3 — Harmony: Focus, Zoom, outcomes

The **Focus** is the exact question the roll must answer. It concerns only what remains uncertain.

State **two possible outcomes**. What the fiction has closed remains closed; what is put into resolution must be able to happen.

Choose the **Zoom** before rolling:

| Zoom | Scale |
| --- | --- |
| **Action** | one gesture or precise moment |
| **Sequence** | a coherent succession of actions |
| **Script** | a broad whole: journey, investigation, operation, prolonged negotiation… |

Zoom does not set difficulty. It determines which dimensions of the fiction remain distinct.

## 4 — Stasis: Frame Factors and Bets

### Frame Factors

A **Frame Factor** closes a possibility, makes a means inadmissible, or imposes a condition. It is neither a bonus nor a penalty.

The fiction must change before a closed possibility can enter the Focus.

### Bets

A **Bet** is an element of the fiction relevant to the current Focus.

- Each Bet gives **one die** on the side of the outcome it favours.
- A Bet may work **against its owner**.
- Count a relevant dimension **only once**: redundant restatements do not give multiple dice.
- There is **no base die**.
- A **0 / N** resolution is valid.
- **0 / 0** is not a roll: return to framing.
- When no one immediately sees another relevant, non-redundant Bet, roll.

### Fate Gauge — optional

Before the roll, if the two sides do not have the same number of Bets, add the **difference** to the Gauge in dice of the colour of the currently disadvantaged side.

The current resolution **does not change**.

Opposing colours in the Gauge cancel each other. Stored dice may be spent later when a difficulty coherent with the fiction appears, according to the authority agreed by the table.

## 5 — Life: roll

Roll one die per Bet on each side.

Dice do not need to remain individually associated with Bets.

Worldviews normally use **D6s**, except Draconic Thought, which uses **D8s**.

## 6 — Disorder: read reality

Read each roll according to its **Worldview**.

For an obstacle with no Worldview of its own:

- **Mirror**: use the same reading as the Protagonist when the trial primarily represents their own overcoming;
- **Middle World**: each **even** die is one Success and each odd die is zero when the obstacle is material or external.

The place or nature of the obstacle may require another reading when the fiction justifies it.

## 7 — Truth: compare

- More Successes: **Victory / Defeat**.
- Tie: **Status quo**.
- A result is **dominated** if both conditions are true:
  1. the difference is **strictly greater than 1** Success;
  2. the winner has **strictly more than twice** the loser's Successes.

Examples: `2–0`, `3–1`, `5–2` are dominated; `4–2`, `6–3` are not.

A dominated result makes a **Feat** available to the winner and/or a **Fiasco** to the loser. The amplification may add a consequence, discovery, transformation, or advantage beyond the two initial outcomes.

### Escalation

On a tie, if the actors refuse the Status quo:

1. keep the existing Bets;
2. each actor who pushes the confrontation adds a Bet representing their determination to end it;
3. resolve again.

## 8 — Illusion: manifest

Describe the result from the Focus, Zoom, announced consequences, two outcomes, and Bets.

All Bets weighed in the balance; they do not all need to appear in the narration of the result.

Lasting consequences become new truths of the fiction and may later become Bets, Frame Factors, or change what is possible.
