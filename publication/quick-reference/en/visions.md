# Worldviews

A Worldview changes **how a roll is read**, not the number of Bets, unless a rule explicitly says otherwise.

The **weakened**, **ordinary**, or **heroic** mode is fixed before the roll according to the fiction and the Frame.

## Theism

### Ordinary

- Each **even** die is one Success.
- Each **6** allows one odd die to be rerolled.
- If the reroll produces another `6`, the support continues.
- If no odd die remains to reroll, a `6` may bring **one new die** into the roll.

### Weakened Worldview — The Silence of the God

- Even dice produce their Successes.
- `6`s trigger **no rerolls**.

### Heroic Worldview — Walking the Myth

- Each **even** die may reroll one odd die.

## Animism

### Ordinary

- Each **even** die is one Success.
- Two dice showing the **same odd value** form a **Spiritual Pact** and together produce **1 Success**.
- A lone odd die produces nothing.

A decisive result may turn a momentary relationship into a lasting one:

- a **Feat** may allow a spirit to be bound into a fetish;
- a **Fiasco** may cause a bound spirit to depart.

### Weakened Worldview — Unravelled Bonds

- Even dice produce their Successes.
- Matching odd dice can no longer form a Spiritual Pact.

### Heroic Worldview — The Circle of Spirits

If **the lowest and highest dice in the roll share the same parity**, every Bet succeeds.

## Logic

### Ordinary

1. Add all the dice.
2. Divide the total by **5**.
3. Each whole group of five is one Success.

### Weakened Worldview — The Imperfect Measure

Divide the total by **6**.

### Heroic Worldview — Convergence toward Law

The divisor progressively decreases:

`4 → 3 → 2 → 1`

## Mysticism

### Ordinary

- Each **even** die is one Success.
- Each `1` may remove **one `6` from the opposing roll**.
- The `1` and `6` disappear together.
- Opposing `6`s are considered only after their own roll has been fully resolved, including rerolls and other effects.

### Weakened Worldview — The Veil of Duality

It takes **two `1`s** to remove a single opposing `6`.

### Heroic Worldview — Erasure of the Self

Choose one of the following two expressions when the fiction establishes it.

#### The Still Center

- The mystic places **no personal Bet**.
- Each Bet on the other side also becomes **one die on the mystic's side**.
- Then resolve the mystical roll normally.

#### The Empty Mirror

- The mystic places Bets normally.
- After the rolls have been resolved, each of their `1`s may take **any die from the opposing roll**.
- The opposing die disappears and the `1` takes its value.

## Draconic Thought

Draconic Bets use **D8s**.

### Ordinary

Each even die (`2`, `4`, `6`, `8`) is one Success.

Then look for complex patterns:

- **The Egg's Stasis**: several dice show the same value;
- **The Ouroboros**: the roll contains both a `1` and an `8`.

For each complex pattern, choose:

#### Wyrm

- the pattern gives **1 additional Success**;
- add or transform a **Bond** or **Negative Attachment** expressing this new fixation on the world.

#### Utuma

- the pattern gives no additional Success;
- the renunciation preserves the dragonewt's spiritual progress.

An abstract obstacle read through Draconic Thought by **Mirror** always uses **Wyrm** for its complex patterns.

### Weakened Worldview — The Grip of the Wyrm

Every complex pattern must be played as **Wyrm**, with its consequences.

### Heroic Worldview — The Awakening of the Dragon

Each complex pattern produces both:

- the Success of **Wyrm**;
- the spiritual benefit of **Utuma**.
